「最慘嘅係，好多人以為買橄欖油只要睇『價錢』同『廣告』就夠。喺超市見到支油包裝靚、標價貴，或者廣告話係『歐洲名產』，就以為買到健康。

但真相係：價錢貴 ≠ 營養高。

如果你只係睇個品牌名，而忽略咗瓶身後面嗰啲『細字標籤』，你好有可能花咗幾百蚊，買返支已經氧化咗、失去多酚營養，甚至係混雜咗低級油嘅『名牌廢油』。最正嘅油如果唔新鮮，或者被高溫存放過，佢嘅營養價值會跌到接近零，甚至變成增加身體發炎嘅來源！

🔍
唔好再被宣傳騙！EVOO 的四個真實層次

而家喺專業油品圈流行嘅睇法係，唔好單純睇『特級初榨 (Extra Virgin)』呢個名，因為呢個只係個「底線」標準，而唔係「頂級」標準。其實 EVOO 可以細分為四個層次：

LEVEL 01
1. 工業級 EVOO

超市大品牌，量產化，產地模糊，酸價接近臨界點 (0.8%)，營養最少，只係勉強合格。

LEVEL 02 · 絕佳 CP 值
2. 區域認證級 EVOO

有 PDO 或 PGI 認證（例如希臘 GAEA），品質穩定，有產地保障，係絕佳嘅高 CP 值選擇。

LEVEL 03 · 生飲保健
3. 精品莊園級 EVOO

單一莊園、原瓶裝瓶（例如 PepperGreen），酸價極低 (0.1% - 0.2%)，多酚含量高，適合生飲。

LEVEL 04 · 極限抗氧化
4. 醫療級/高多酚 EVOO

極端環境生長（例如 Atlas 摩洛哥），多酚含量爆表 (600mg+)，專為強效細胞抗發炎而設。

⚠️ 如果你只係睇價錢，你可能會用『醫療級』嘅價錢買到『工業級』嘅油！
🎯
超市生存指南：點樣用 CP 值思維揀「真新鮮」？

喺香港超級市場買油，唔好睇宣傳，請直接翻轉支油睇「後便標籤」同「關鍵重點」：

1

睇「酸價 (Acidity)」 ➔ 新鮮度嘅唯一指標

重點： 酸價越低 ➔ 橄欖採收後越快壓榨 ➔ 橄欖越新鮮 ➔ 營養越高。

💡 CP 值選擇：如果標籤寫住酸價 ≤ 0.2% 或 0.3%，已經係非常之高質。如果你見到酸價接近 0.8%，就算支油幾貴都唔好買。
2

睇「原瓶裝瓶 (Estate Bottled)」

重點： 避開嗰啲「多國混釀」或「第三方分裝」嘅油。

💡 CP 值選擇：搵標明單一產區或單一莊園嘅。因為跨國運輸分裝最容易造成氧化同混入劣質油。
3

睇「包裝材質」 ➔ 阻絕光害

重點： 橄欖油最怕光，UV 光線會迅速分解葉綠素並加速油脂氧化。

💡 CP 值選擇：絕對唔好買透明玻璃樽！一定要揀深色玻璃樽或錫罐。如果係透明樽，就算入面啲油幾頂級，擺喺超市燈光下兩星期就氧化晒。
4

睇「採收日期 (Harvest Date)」

重點： 橄欖油唔係葡萄酒，唔係越陳越好，而係越新鮮越好！

💡 CP 值選擇：揀最近一年內採收並冷壓壓榨嘅油。
🛒 香港超市揀油公式
高 CP 值 = 深色樽 ＋ 單一產區 ＋ 低酸價 (≤ 0.3%) ＋ 近期採收

唔好再俾個品牌名或者貴價錢蒙蔽。下次去超市，請翻轉支油，用呢個公式去篩選。記住，健康係睇「數據」同「標籤」，而唔係睇「廣告」！

⚠️ 最重要嘅警告：營養 vs 高溫 🌡️

呢部分係好多人最容易做錯嘅地方！請記住：橄欖油係有營養，但高溫會令佢變廢油。很多人以為買咗貴油，就可以用嚟大火爆炒，呢個係大錯特錯！

  • 營養嘅秘密： 橄欖油入面最珍貴嘅「多酚」同「維生素」，都係極之怕熱嘅。
  • 高溫陷阱： 當你用高溫大火爆炒，油溫超過咗佢嘅發煙點，啲營養物質會迅速分解，甚至產生有害氧化自由基。
  • 殘酷結果： 你花咗幾百蚊買支頂級油，但如果用嚟大火炸雞，最後你飲入肚嘅只係一種「冇營養嘅脂肪」，完全失去咗抗氧化嘅功效。

Opening Reality Check: Buying Expensive Rancid Waste Oil?

Many consumers believe that buying healthy olive oil simply requires looking at the brand name, aesthetic packaging, or high price tag.

The Cold Truth: High Price ≠ High Nutrition.

If you judge olive oil solely by the marketing front label and overlook the back fine print, you might be paying top dollar for oil that has already oxidized, lost its polyphenol vitality, or been blended with commercial filler oils. Rancid oil promotes systemic inflammation rather than squelching it!

🔍
The 4 Real Tiers of Extra Virgin Olive Oil

"Extra Virgin" is merely the baseline legal threshold, not the gold standard. Here is the insider classification:

LEVEL 01
1. Industrial EVOO

Mass-produced supermarket giants, blended origins, acidity hovering near legal ceiling (0.8%). Bare minimum nutrition.

LEVEL 02 · Best Value
2. Regionally Certified (PDO/PGI)

Guaranteed protected origin (e.g., GAEA Greece), strict harvest controls, exceptional cost-performance balance.

LEVEL 03 · Daily Wellness
3. Single Estate Bottled

Single-estate harvest and bottling (e.g., PepperGreen). Ultra-low acidity (0.1%–0.2%), dense polyphenols, perfect for raw shots.

LEVEL 04 · High Polyphenol
4. Medicinal High-Polyphenol

Cultivated under extreme arid conditions (e.g., Atlas Morocco), massive polyphenol density (600mg/kg+), formulated for therapeutic cellular recovery.

🎯
Supermarket Survival Guide: 4 Rules of True Freshness
1

Check "Acidity" ➔ The Definitive Metric of Freshness

Lower acidity = faster processing after harvest = pristine cellular nutrition.

💡 Benchmark: Acidity ≤ 0.2% or 0.3% denotes premium freshness. Skip anything approaching 0.8%.
2

Look for "Estate Bottled" (Single Origin)

Avoid multi-country blends or third-party rebottling, which invite oxidation and adulteration during transit.

💡 Benchmark: Choose single-estate or designated single-region producers.
3

Inspect the Packaging ➔ Absolute Light Shielding

UV radiation degrades chlorophyll and rapidly oxidizes healthy fatty acids.

💡 Benchmark: Never buy clear glass bottles. Insist on dark amber/green glass or tin containers.
4

Verify Harvest Date

Unlike vintage wine, olive oil degrades over time. Fresher is unequivocally superior.

💡 Benchmark: Harvested and cold-pressed within the preceding 12 months.
🛒 Supermarket EVOO Formula
Peak ROI = Dark Bottle ＋ Single Estate ＋ Low Acidity (≤ 0.3%) ＋ Recent Harvest

Evaluate olive oil by biochemical markers and label veracity, never by persuasive marketing.

⚠️ Critical Warning: Nutrition vs. High Heat 🌡️

Top-grade olive oil is designed for cellular wellness, never for high-heat deep frying!

  • Polyphenol Vulnerability: Polyphenols and delicate antioxidants degrade abruptly when subjected to high temperatures.
  • Smoke Point Degradation: Exceeding smoke points creates toxic lipid peroxides and aldehydes.
  • Nutritional Waste: Using a $50 artisanal EVOO for deep frying converts pristine medicine into stripped, inert fat.
💡 Proper Cooking Applications:

🌿 Raw / Dressing

Reserve elite EVOO for direct shots or cool drizzling to capture 100% of bioactive polyphenols.

🍳 Gentle Sautéing

Low-temperature simmers or brief pan-warming below smoke point.

🔥 High Heat Wok Frying

Deploy high-smoke-point avocado oil or grass-fed beef tallow instead.
